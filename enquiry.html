This agreement applies only to the equipment listed in this Gear Hire enquiry. Sending this enquiry does not itself confirm the booking. The agreement records the terms that will apply if OzzSound accepts and supplies the hire.

Read the Gear Hire terms

1. Equipment and ownership

The equipment, cases, covers, cables, stands, adapters and accessories supplied by OzzSound remain the property of OzzSound. The hirer may possess and use them only for the agreed hire period and intended purpose and must not sell, sub-hire, pledge, modify, dismantle or repair them without OzzSound's permission.

2. Testing and condition

OzzSound will take reasonable steps to inspect and test the equipment before handover. The hirer should inspect the equipment on receipt and promptly report visible damage, missing items or apparent faults. Pre-existing damage and fair wear and tear are not the hirer's responsibility.

3. Care and responsibility

From handover until return, the hirer must take reasonable care of the equipment, keep it secure and protect it from theft, misuse, liquids, weather, excessive moisture, heat, impact and other avoidable hazards. Equipment must be used safely and for its intended purpose.

4. Faults during hire

If equipment develops a fault, the hirer must stop using it where continued use could create a safety risk or further damage and contact OzzSound as soon as reasonably practicable. A genuine equipment failure not caused or contributed to by the hirer does not automatically make the hirer responsible for repair or replacement.

5. Damage, loss and investigation

If equipment is returned damaged, non-operational, incomplete, lost or stolen, OzzSound may investigate the circumstances. OzzSound may consider testing, photographs, condition records, technician or repairer findings and the hirer's explanation. The hirer will be given a reasonable opportunity to provide relevant information before a final damage claim is made.

6. Repair or replacement costs

Where OzzSound reasonably determines that loss or damage was caused or contributed to by the hirer, a person permitted by the hirer to use the equipment, misuse, negligence or a breach of this agreement, the hirer may be responsible for the reasonable inspection, repair, parts or replacement costs arising from that loss or damage. If equipment is lost, destroyed or beyond economical repair in circumstances for which the hirer is responsible, OzzSound may seek the reasonable replacement cost or other reasonable loss permitted by law. These amounts are compensatory and are not intended as a penalty.

7. Missing items and cleaning

All supplied equipment and accessories must be returned. Reasonable replacement costs may apply to missing items. Reasonable cleaning costs may apply where substantial cleaning is required because of spills, excessive dirt, smoke residue, food, drink, mud or other contamination beyond ordinary use.

8. Late return

The hirer must return equipment by the agreed time unless OzzSound approves an extension. Reasonable additional hire charges may apply to an unapproved late return.

9. Smoke, fog, haze and alarm-sensitive equipment

Where the hirer selects a smoke machine, fog machine, haze machine, atmospheric effect or other equipment that may activate or interfere with smoke detectors, fire alarms, security systems, ventilation systems or other building systems, the hirer is responsible for checking the intended location and obtaining any required approval from the venue owner, manager or authorised person before the hire begins. OzzSound does not represent that such equipment is permitted at a particular venue merely because it is available for hire.

If the hirer later discovers that the venue does not permit the equipment, or chooses not to use it because of venue restrictions, alarms, detectors or site conditions, the agreed hire charge for that equipment remains payable where OzzSound supplied the equipment as agreed and it was otherwise fit for the hire. The hirer must not disable, cover, isolate or interfere with fire alarms, smoke detectors or other safety systems unless lawfully undertaken by an authorised person.

10. Strobes and special effects

The hirer is responsible for considering whether strobe or flashing effects are appropriate for the event and attendees and for following applicable venue requirements and OzzSound safety instructions.

11. Collection and return inspection

OzzSound may inspect and test equipment after return. Acceptance of returned equipment does not prevent OzzSound from raising a subsequently discovered concealed or non-obvious issue that could not reasonably have been identified during the initial return inspection.

12. Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this agreement excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee, right, remedy or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified under the Australian Consumer Law.

13. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws applicable in Tasmania, Australia. If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to operate to the extent permitted by law.
