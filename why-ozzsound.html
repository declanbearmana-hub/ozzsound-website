Built around your event

Weddings, school functions, birthdays, corporate events, sporting celebrations, karaoke and something completely different all need a different approach. Ozzsound adapts the music, setup and entertainment to suit the occasion.

As formal or as fun as you want

Some events call for a polished background presence. Others work best when the DJ gets involved with the crowd, hosts games or competitions and helps create the energy in the room. The style can be shaped around your event.

Planning beyond the playlist

Sound, lighting, venue layout, timing and the flow of the event all matter. Ozzsound's planning tools help you think through the experience before the day, while final setup decisions remain tailored to the venue and event requirements.
