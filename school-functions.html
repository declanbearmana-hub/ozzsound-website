Tasmanian school requirements

Credentials & compliance.

Tasmanian government schools require contractors and external providers working with children to maintain a current Tasmanian Registration to Work with Vulnerable People (RWVP).

Schools may also have their own sign-in, induction, risk-management or contractor documentation requirements. Let us know what your school needs as part of the enquiry.

Important: a White Card is a construction-induction credential for construction work. It is separate from RWVP and is not a substitute for child-safety registration.
