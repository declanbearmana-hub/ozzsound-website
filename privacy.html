Your Privacy

This policy explains how Ozzsound Mobile Music handles personal information supplied through the website, enquiries, bookings and event planning.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Ozzsound, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, event date, venue or suburb, event details, guest numbers, music and setup preferences, enquiry and booking information, and photos or files that you choose to upload.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, plan and manage events, confirm availability and bookings, communicate with customers, provide the customer portal, arrange equipment or entertainment requirements, and maintain appropriate business records.

Photos and uploads

Only upload photos or files that you are authorised to provide and that are relevant to your event. Uploaded material may be used by Ozzsound for event planning, setup visualisation or the specific purpose explained when you upload it. Ozzsound does not treat an event-planning upload as permission to publish it publicly.

Storage and service providers

Ozzsound uses third-party technology providers to operate parts of the website and business systems, including secure database, storage, website hosting and email-delivery services. Information may be processed or stored using those services where reasonably necessary to operate Ozzsound's website, enquiries and bookings.

Sharing your information

Ozzsound does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to provide or administer the service, where you have authorised it, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Keeping information secure

Ozzsound takes reasonable steps to protect information held through its website and business systems. No internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid submitting information that is not required for your enquiry or event.

Access, correction and privacy questions

If you want to ask what information Ozzsound holds about you, request a correction, raise a privacy concern, or ask about deletion where applicable, contact Ozzsound at ozzsound@hotmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Ozzsound's website, services or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page with its updated date.
