Meet Dazz.
Endless energy.

Decades of music. One genuine passion.

For Dazz, music has never been just a job. It’s been a way of life. With more than four decades behind the decks, he brings experience, personality and a love of entertaining to every event.

From vinyl to digital

Four decades.
Still loving every beat.

Born in 1965, Dazz started DJing at just 17 — around 1982. He began with vinyl records and has grown with the music, equipment and entertainment trends ever since.

From record crates to modern computer-based DJ systems, the technology has changed. His enthusiasm hasn’t. Generations of dancefloors have taught him how to read a crowd and shape the music around the people in front of him.

  1. Around 1982

    The first records

    A teenager with a love of music starts his journey behind the decks.

  2. Through the decades

    Moving with the music

    New genres, new equipment and different crowds. Always learning, always entertaining.

  3. Today & beyond

    Digital decks. Same passion.

    More than four decades of experience, with plenty of music still to share.

Personality comes included

A big smile.
A room full of energy.

Known for his bubbly nature, positive attitude and infectious enthusiasm, Dazz loves getting involved and helping people enjoy themselves.

He’s just as comfortable on the microphone as an MC as he is behind the DJ desk. From a polished corporate function to a lively school disco, he adapts to the occasion and the crowd.

Experience across the board

So many crowds.
So many good nights.

  • Charity events & fundraisers
  • Sporting events & MC hosting
  • School discos & formals
  • Corporate celebrations
  • Bingo nights
  • Weddings, birthdays & parties

Whatever the function, Dazz brings the same care: music, atmosphere and entertainment shaped around your event.

The reason he keeps going

His livelihood.
His hobby.
His passion.

After all these years, Dazz still loves what he does. He wants every event to be memorable and hopes to keep bringing people together through music for many years to come.

His ambition is to keep doing what he loves and continue building his name as one of Tasmania’s best-known DJs.
